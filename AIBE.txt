## Role

Act as an expert **AIBE examination strategist, Indian legal educator, legal-question-bank designer, and instructional-content architect**.

I am an **LLB student preparing for the All India Bar Examination (AIBE) XXI 2026**. Create a comprehensive, rigorous, exam-oriented **AIBE XXI preparation system** using the two supplied PDF documents as the authoritative references:

1. **`AIBE_XXI_English_Set_A.pdf`** — use this as the primary reference for understanding the style, structure, difficulty, wording, subject distribution, and question patterns of the examination.
2. **`Syllabus for All India Bar Exam-XXI.pdf`** — use this as the authoritative source for the AIBE XXI syllabus and subject coverage.

Do **not** assume that information belongs to the AIBE XXI syllabus merely because it appeared in an older AIBE examination. Clearly distinguish between:

* syllabus-derived content,
* content inferred from the previous question paper,
* general Indian legal knowledge, and
* questions created for practice.

# 4. Flashpoints

After every major topic, create a **FLASHPOINTS** section.

Flashpoints should be extremely concise, memorable examination facts such as:

* "Article X → principle Y"
* "Section X → rule Y"
* "Case A → doctrine B"
* "Exception → condition"
* "Limitation period → X"
* "Authority → power"
* "Provision A ≠ Provision B"

Use visual emphasis and short memory-oriented statements.

---

# 5. Create a Question Bank of MORE THAN 500 MCQs

Create **at least 600 MCQs**, preferably distributed proportionately across all syllabus subjects.

Do not generate 500 superficial questions by merely changing names or numbers.

The question bank should test different cognitive levels:

### Level 1 — Recall

Definitions, sections, articles, dates, terminology and basic principles.

### Level 2 — Understanding

Conceptual understanding and distinctions.

### Level 3 — Application

Apply a legal principle to a factual situation.

### Level 4 — Analysis

Compare provisions, identify exceptions, analyse competing principles and solve legal problems.

Aim for a meaningful mixture of all four levels, with substantial emphasis on Levels 2–4.

# 7. Assertion–Reason Questions

Include a substantial dedicated section of **Assertion–Reason questions**.

Use this standard format:

**Assertion (A):** ...

**Reason (R):** ...

Choose:

A. Both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A
B. Both A and R are true, but R does not correctly explain A
C. A is true, but R is false
D. A is false, but R is true

For every question provide:

* Correct answer
* Explanation of Assertion
* Explanation of Reason
* Whether R explains A
* Relevant legal provision/case
* Flashpoint

Do not make the answer predictable based on wording.

---

# 9. Answer Randomisation — VERY IMPORTANT

Randomise correct answers across A, B, C and D.

The answer distribution must NOT systematically favour any option.

Across the entire question bank:

* A ≈ 25%
* B ≈ 25%
* C ≈ 25%
* D ≈ 25%

Allow only minor statistical variation.

Also ensure:

* No obvious repeating sequence
* No pattern such as A-B-C-D-A-B-C-D
* No subject-specific answer bias
* No difficulty-level answer bias
* No assertion–reason answer bias

Before finalising the question bank, programmatically calculate the distribution of correct answers and rebalance it if necessary.

Display an **Answer Distribution Audit** showing:

| Option | Count | Percentage |
| ------ | ----: | ---------: |
| A      |       |            |
| B      |       |            |
| C      |       |            |
| D      |       |            |

# 10. Avoid Poor Question Design

Do NOT create:

* Ambiguous questions
* Two technically correct options
* Questions where the answer is obvious from wording
* Unnecessarily obscure trivia
* Duplicate questions
* Near-duplicate questions
* Artificially complicated language
* Incorrect legal propositions
* Outdated law presented as current law
* Questions outside the AIBE XXI syllabus unless explicitly labelled as supplementary
* Distractors that are obviously absurd

Every question must have **one defensible correct answer**.

# 11. Question Quality Control

After generating the questions, perform an internal quality audit.

Check every question for:

### Legal Accuracy

Is the legal proposition correct?

### Syllabus Alignment

Does it belong to the AIBE XXI syllabus?

### Uniqueness

Is it materially different from existing questions?

### Answer Validity

Is there exactly one correct answer?

### Distractor Quality

Are the incorrect answers plausible?

### Explanation Quality

Does the explanation actually establish why the answer is correct?

### Current Law

Where relevant, ensure that the proposition reflects the law applicable for the AIBE XXI preparation period.

### Source Traceability

Identify the source basis:

* Official syllabus
* Supplied AIBE question paper
* Relevant statute/bare Act
* Constitutional provision
* Judicial decision
* Other authoritative legal source
